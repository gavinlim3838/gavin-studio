Why it fails

Why most websites don't convert.

Traffic isn't the problem. Leaks are. These four show up in almost every site I audit — and every one of them is fixable.

No clear value

Visitors can't tell what you do or why it matters in the first 5 seconds.

Weak structure

Key information is buried, scattered or in the wrong order.

No clear call to action

People like what they see — then leave, because nobody told them what to do next.

No follow-up system

Leads go cold the moment they close the tab. No emails, no WhatsApp, no second chance.

FAQ

Good questions.

How long does a project take?

Most landing pages go live in about a week, full funnels and websites in one to two weeks — depending on how quickly content and feedback come in.

Do I need to write the copy myself?

No. I handle the structure and copywriting based on our discovery call. You just review and approve.

Which platform will you build on?

Whatever fits your business — GoHighLevel, ClickFunnels, WordPress, Webflow or a custom-coded site. If you already use a platform, I'll build inside it.

Can you connect WhatsApp and email follow-up?

Yes. Automated email sequences, WhatsApp messages and CRM pipelines are part of the system, so every lead gets followed up instantly.

Do you work with clients internationally?

Yes. I work with coaches, trainers and service businesses in the US, Canada, Australia and the UK, fully remote.
